## 4. Inconsistencies and Minor Discrepancies Noted

1. **`best_val_loss` Metadata Field in `latest_checkpoint.pt`:**
   - **Observation:** In `latest_checkpoint.pt`, the metadata field `"best_val_loss"` is recorded as `10.184041 K` (the previous best from Epoch 3), whereas in `best_model.pt` and `training_history.json`, it is recorded as `10.164977 K` (Epoch 5).
   - **Root Cause:** In [`scripts/train_meteor_net.py`](file:///c:/Users/Krishna/OneDrive/Documents/CS%20CORE%20SUBJECTS%20NOTES/PROJECTS/MAJOR%20PROJECT/scripts/train_meteor_net.py) (lines 465–476), `latest_checkpoint.pt` was serialized immediately before the evaluation condition `if epoch_val_loss < best_val_loss:`. At the moment `latest_checkpoint.pt` was written, `best_val_loss` had not yet been updated with Epoch 5's new lowest value.
   - **Impact Assessment:** **Zero impact on model weights or training validity.** Both checkpoints contain identical Epoch 5 weights, and `best_model.pt` accurately reflects `10.164977 K`.
2. **Missing Files:**
   - **None.** All planned artifacts (`baseline_metrics.json`, `best_model.pt`, `latest_checkpoint.pt`, `training_history.json`, `training_schedule_provenance.json`, `patch_classification_audit.json`) exist, are non-empty, and load without error.
