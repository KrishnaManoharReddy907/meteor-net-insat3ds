H. Regional Transfer Generalization Experiments
To evaluate the generalization performance of the model
trained over the central China region when applied to temporal
super-resolution reconstruction over more complex terrain
and diﬀerent climatic zones, we conduct a set of regional
transfer generalization experiments. As shown in Fig. 11, four
representative subregions are selected: 1) the Yunnan–Guizhou
Plateau (complex high-elevation mountainous terrain); 2) the
Yangtze River Delta coastal lowlands (land-sea transition
zone); 3) a South China heavy-rainfall region around the
Pearl River Delta (frequent severe convection and torrential
rainfall); and 4) the semi-arid boundary region along the
northern/western margins of the Loess Plateau. These subre-
gions jointly cover typical combinations of terrain and climate
from plateau to coastal areas and from humid to semi-arid
environments.

resolution reconstruction performance of METEOR-Net and
